About the clinic

Dentistry Designed Around You

Aira Multispeciality Dental Clinic started in Bardhaman in 2011 with one simple belief — dentistry should be comfortable, honest and personal. We brought specialist treatment and modern technology to a neighbourhood clinic, so families of Burdwan district no longer need to travel to Kolkata for quality dental care.

Every treatment begins with listening. Your dentist will show you your digital X-rays on screen, explain each option in simple language and give you a clear, itemised estimate before treatment starts — no hidden charges, ever.

Our mission is to make advanced dental care affordable and reassuring for the families of Burdwan district — so that every patient goes home with healthier teeth and a more confident smile.

For most adults, a check-up and cleaning every six months is enough to keep the gums and enamel healthy. If you have gum disease, implants, heavy wear or a higher risk of decay, we usually advise a review every three to four months, so that small changes are caught early.

Yes, when it is done under a dentist's supervision. We first check the health of your gums and old fillings, and then use clinically approved whitening gels with protective barriers. Sensitivity is usually mild and settles within a day or two, and we adjust the strength as per your enamel and comfort level.

The placement itself takes about 45 to 60 minutes under local anaesthesia. The implant then fuses with the jawbone over three to four months before the final crown is fixed. In some cases teeth can be provided sooner — the CBCT scan taken during your first visit will tell us whether you are suitable.
